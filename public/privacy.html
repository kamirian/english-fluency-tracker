Privacy

English Fluency Tracker · Last updated October 2026

Without an account

If you use the tracker without signing in, your progress is stored only in your browser on that device. The site does not receive it.

When you sign in with Google

Google shares your name, email address, profile picture link, and a Google account identifier. The tracker uses them to recognize you and keep your progress separate from everyone else's. It does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

  • Your tracker data (check-ins, learning library, writing, practice sessions, reviews, and your learner profile) is stored in a database so it can sync across your devices.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Only a one-way hash of it is stored on the server.

AI connections

If you connect an AI provider, your API key is encrypted before it is stored and is never shown again. When you use an AI feature, the relevant text (for example, your practice conversation or a writing draft) and your learner profile are sent to the provider you chose, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your account with that provider.

Copy-prompt mode sends nothing through this site: you copy the prompt into any AI chat yourself and paste the result back.

What is not collected

No advertising, no third-party analytics, and no selling of data. Voice clips you record stay in your browser unless you download and share them yourself.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, synced progress, and saved AI connections from the server, and clears your progress from that browser. You can download a backup first in Settings → Backup.

Hosting

The tracker runs on Cloudflare. Like any website, requests pass through Cloudflare's network, which may keep routine technical logs.
